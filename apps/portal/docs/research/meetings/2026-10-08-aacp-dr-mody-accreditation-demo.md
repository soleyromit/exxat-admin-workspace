## ACPE self-study structure (research)

- **7 standards** (2025 edition — changed from 25 standards to 7)
  - Standard 1: Organization and Governance
  - Standard 2: Curriculum
  - Standard 3: Interprofessional Education
  - Standard 4: Experiential Learning (IPPs, APPs)
  - Standard 5: Preceptors
  - Standard 6: Students and Student Services
  - Standard 7: Assessment (assesses Standards 1–6)
- Each standard has subsections; each subsection has sub-subsections (e.g., 1.1.a – 1.1.f)
- Self-study runs ~1.5 years; submission to ACPE is then reviewed

## FARMS portal structure (research — portal mirrors guidance document)

Per standard/subsection:
- **Narrative text field** (character-limited, e.g., 7,500 characters for college overview)
- **Required document upload** (mandatory evidence, e.g., org chart, faculty CVs, NAPLEX report)
- **Optional document upload** (supporting evidence)
- **Rating radio buttons:** Commendable / Meets Expectation / Needs Improvement / Unsatisfactory
- Overall compliance per standard: Compliant / Compliant with monitoring / Partially compliant / Non-compliant

## Role access structure

- **Steering committee** (3 people: not the dean — dean reviews final draft, doesn't run process)
- **Standard leaders** (one per standard, plus deputy, working group members)
- **Faculty** (contribute per their standard assignment)
- **Students** (ex officio, no voting rights; shown collective non-identifying data only; excluded from GPA discussions)
- **Dean** reviews and formally submits the final report

## Yearly reporting triggers (threshold-based, not scheduled)

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| Attrition rate > 24% | Report to ACPE |
| NAPLEX score < 60% or > 2 SD from national average | Report to ACPE |
| Class size change > 20% | Report to ACPE |
| Change in senior leadership | Report to ACPE |
| Standard monitoring status | Regular data updates to ACPE |

## Dr. Modi's recommendation for Exxat (stakeholder input)

> "I don't know if the addition of the portal is going to be that helpful for the faculty unless you provide another option that the report will be automatically generated."

> "You can develop a tool for helping assess, you know, exams or the AACP surveys. Those actually will be much more beneficial for pharmacy."

Key takeaway: **Focus on analytics (AACP faculty survey analysis, NAPLEX comparisons vs. peers and national averages) rather than replicating FARMS.** Pharmacy already has FARMS. Other disciplines don't have a portal — for those, a collection/organization tool could be valuable.

## Discipline spectrum for accreditation portals (research)

| Discipline | Prescriptiveness |
|---|---|
| PT (Physical Therapy) | Very prescriptive — gives reporting templates |
| Pharmacy (ACPE) | Prescriptive on required evidence; flexible on format |
| Nursing/PA | Less prescriptive — schools choose formats |

## Implication for product design (flag — not a directive)

If building an accreditation module:
- For pharmacy: value is analytics (survey trends, NAPLEX, cohort data), not portal replication
- For other disciplines: a structured evidence-collection tool (mimicking FARMS pattern) may have value
- Role-based access model: steering committee + standard leaders + faculty (contributing) + students (ex officio, limited view)
- Yearly threshold-based reporting is a distinct workflow (not just self-study)
