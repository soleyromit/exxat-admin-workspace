## A. What was said, in one paragraph each

1. **Today's workaround.** Schools track skills only through eval forms in Prism. Form type 1 is a long binary checklist (50 to 100 questions, grouped in sections, faculty drives it, no student part). Form type 2 is a self-evaluation form: the student multi-selects skills, self-rates, sends to faculty, preceptor or lab technician, who see only the selected skills and add their own evaluation. Lab technicians and external preceptors are added as placement faculty with full access (no delegator link). Schools create a dummy "skills tracking course" and register the whole cohort. The skill list lives inside the form; the CI team builds it per school. `[CALL 10-26]`
2. **Two configuration dimensions per skill.** (a) Evaluation criteria: approved or not approved; several questions with a final approved or not approved; independence level (independent, with assistance, observed). (b) Frequency threshold: how many valid attempts before the skill is met, school-defined (3 approvals, 3 independent). A student may attempt 5 times, 2 not approved, so 3 count. A threshold can be question-based: 2 of 3 sub-questions answered yes counts as one valid attempt. `[CALL 10-26]`
3. **Library.** One central module; schools name the items whatever they want (skills, competencies, EPAs). Phase 1: manual entry, or the CI team bulk-adds once on their behalf. Phase 2, later and out of scope now: AI or document import. Competencies and standards must be separated (standards are managed elsewhere). `[CALL 10-26]`
4. **Assignment.** Define the skills once and tag them to a cohort (class of 2027); when a course offering is created for that cohort, the skills flow to all its clinical offerings. On top of that, per course: mandatory, recommended, optional. Non-lockstep programs have no cohort: schools need their own student groups (filter on category, enrollment year, graduation year), useful beyond skills. How to map cross-year groups to courses is OPEN; Sankalp asks Vishakha on Oct 27. Whether to start with clinical courses only (not didactic) is a question for Arthi. `[CALL 10-26]`
5. **Reporting.** Student view (top and bottom performers, drill into which skills a student has or has not completed and in which rotation) and skills view (which skills students struggle with, to tell lack of exposure from a complex skill). Sarah's past tool also showed a faculty by location heat map; its data source is unclear and part of it is USC-specific. Remediation stays outside the system for now (advisement module). Reporting is deferred for Thursday. `[CALL 10-26]`
6. **Prototype and Thursday.** Show only a curated set of marquee screens: setup (skills library creation, assignment), student submission, reviewer or preceptor evaluation. Tell Arthi reporting is not ready. Internal items (tier labels, build status, the lab bar) are not for Arthi. Sankalp was confused that the dashboard had 6 variants in the nav and 3 in the bar. Sankalp sends a curated list by Oct 27; Wednesday sync; weekly Thursday calls. `[CALL 10-26]`
